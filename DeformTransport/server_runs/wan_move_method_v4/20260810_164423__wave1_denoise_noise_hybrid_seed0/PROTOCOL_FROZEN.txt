V4A-D20:
For denoising steps 0..7 only,
compute clean estimate x0 = z_t - sigma_t * v_t,
apply deterministic V3D material transport to x0,
then convert back using v'_t = (z_t - x0') / sigma_t.
Center latent cells only. Hard intervention. No alpha sweep.

V4A-D40:
Same as V4A-D20 but denoising steps 0..15.
No result-dependent duration tuning.

V4B-NOISE:
Before denoising, transport the initial Gaussian noise along
persistent material trajectories.
Depth-aware deterministic z-buffer.
3x3 local noise support.
Fixed interpolation:
noise' = noise + 0.2 * (T(noise) - noise).
No theta sweep.

V4C-HYBRID:
V4B-NOISE plus V4A-D20.

No candidate parameters may be changed after inspecting generated results.

   on either development case;
